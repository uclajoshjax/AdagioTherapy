Therapy for Salem and Keizer, Oregon

I know Salem well: from 2013 to 2018 I created and directed the behavioral health program at Willamette Family Medical Center, working closely with doctors and families in the community. Today I offer secure video therapy to Salem, Keizer, and nearby Willamette Valley towns.

I see individuals for trauma, anxiety, and depression using IFS and EMDR, and couples using Emotionally Focused Therapy. Kaiser Permanente members need no referral.
