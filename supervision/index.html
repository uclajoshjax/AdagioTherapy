Clinical supervision for MSW associates

I offer ongoing supervision for MSW associates working toward their licensure hours. I have led a clinical department, supervised psychologists, LPCs, LCSWs, and student interns, and worked across hospital, primary care, trauma, and private practice settings.

What supervision looks like

  • Case conceptualization and treatment planning
  • Trauma-informed and attachment-based practice
  • Ethics, documentation, and boundaries
  • Building a sustainable career, including private practice

I enjoy helping new clinicians grow. Email me about availability, format, and fees.
