Fees and insurance

  • Fee: $200 per session for individuals and couples
  • Sliding scale: a few spots are available; ask about eligibility
  • Free consultation: 15 minutes by phone or in person
  • Payment: card, HSA, cash, or check
  • Appointments and copays: view them in the client portal

Insurance I accept

  • Kaiser Permanente: in network, no referral needed (my Kaiser clinician profile)
  • Regence Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • PacificSource Commercial (my PacificSource provider listing)
  • Oregon Health Plan (OHP / Medicaid)
  • First Choice Health (FCH)
  • Samaritan Health

Other plans: call or email and I will let you know. Coverage for couples sessions and telehealth depends on your plan, and I am happy to help you check.

Your first session and insurance

To bill insurance, I need enough clinical background to make a diagnosis so that I can keep seeing you under your plan. Your first session is a structured assessment with questions beyond your intake forms, and I will explain what I am asking and why.
